---
title: "2. Medium Priority"
description: Real wins, smaller or narrower than the first chapter.
---

These matter, but each applies to fewer pages or moves a smaller number than
[the high-priority items](/frontend-performance). Work through them once
those are done.

Several are about *layout stability* rather than raw speed —
[image dimensions](/frontend-performance/image-dimensions) and
[lazy loading](/frontend-performance/lazy-loading) both affect whether the page
stays still while it loads, which users feel as much as they feel load time.
