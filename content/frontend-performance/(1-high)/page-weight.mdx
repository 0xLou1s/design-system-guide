> Keep your page weight < 1500 KB (ideally < 500 kb)

Total transferred bytes for the page and everything it pulls in. The 1500kB
figure is roughly the web median, which makes it a low bar — 500kB is the
target worth aiming at.

This is a *symptom* metric. You do not fix page weight directly; you fix it by
fixing [images](/frontend-performance/compress-images),
[JavaScript](/frontend-performance/minify-javascript) and
[fonts](/frontend-performance/web-font-size), which is where the bytes almost
always are.

## Where to look

Open the Network panel, hard-reload, and sort by size. In practice the answer is
nearly always one of:

- An unoptimized hero image, often several hundred kB on its own.
- A JavaScript bundle carrying a dependency nobody needed.
- Font files, especially when several weights load.
- An autoplaying video, which can dwarf everything else.

## Why it matters beyond speed

Bytes cost money on metered connections. A 3MB page is a real expense for
someone on a prepaid mobile plan, and that is a substantial share of users in
much of the world.
