---
title: "1. High Priority"
description: The items that move the numbers most, and that almost every site gets wrong.
---

These are the ones to fix first. Between them they account for most of the
difference between a fast page and a slow one, and most sites have several
outstanding.

They fall into three rough clusters:

**Targets** — page weight, load time and TTFB. Not fixes in themselves, but the
numbers the rest of the work moves. Start by measuring them.

**Delivery** — compression, caching, request count, protocol. Server-side
settings, usually a one-time configuration change, usually the cheapest wins
available.

**Assets** — CSS, JavaScript and images. Where the bytes actually are.

<Callout>
The priority tiers come from the original checklist. They are a reasonable
default ordering, but your page is not the average page — measure before
assuming the high-priority items are where your problem is.
</Callout>
