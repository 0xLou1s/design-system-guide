> Lighthouse

Built into Chrome DevTools, also available as a CLI and a CI action. It loads
the page under a simulated slow network and CPU, then scores performance,
accessibility, SEO and best practices.

Use it for a first look at an unfamiliar page, and for catching regressions in
CI.

## Treat the score with caution

The number is a weighted composite of several metrics from **one run under
simulated conditions**. Scores bounce by several points between runs on the same
unchanged page.

**Treat a 5-point change as noise.** Read the underlying metrics and the
opportunities list instead — those are specific and actionable in a way the
score never is.

The score is also easy to optimize *for* rather than optimizing the page. A site
can score 100 and still feel slow to real users, which is why
[field data](/frontend-performance/pagespeed-insights) matters more.

## In CI

Lighthouse CI runs the audit on every PR and fails the build when a metric or
budget regresses:

```yaml
- uses: treosh/lighthouse-ci-action@v12
  with:
    urls: https://staging.example.com/
    budgetPath: ./budget.json
```

Assert on **specific metrics** rather than the overall score — LCP and total
blocking time are stable enough to gate on; the composite is not.

Run it several times and take the median. A single run in CI produces flaky
builds and teaches everyone to ignore the check.
