---
title: "3. Low Priority"
description: Narrower wins, and the items whose priority has shifted most.
---

Lower priority in the original checklist — but "low" here means *narrower in
scope*, not *unimportant*. Two things are worth saying about this chapter.

**The font items are underrated.** [WOFF2](/frontend-performance/woff2),
[font size](/frontend-performance/web-font-size) and
[flash of invisible text](/frontend-performance/flash-text) sit in low priority,
but fonts are render-blocking by nature and a mistake here is visible on every
page load. On a text-heavy site these belong higher.

**One item has aged out.**
[CSS concatenation](/frontend-performance/concatenate-css) was essential under
HTTP/1.1 and is now a trade-off that often goes the other way. It is kept here
for completeness, with an explanation of when it still applies.
