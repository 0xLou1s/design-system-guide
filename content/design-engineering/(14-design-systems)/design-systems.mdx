A design system is a mechanism for making a decision once and having it apply everywhere. Design engineering is usually the role that owns the mechanism.

This is the most common way the job appears in a posting — product design engineers working on design systems, UI components, shipping directly to product.

It is also where your engineering background is least of a handicap and most of an advantage. A design system is a library with users, versions and a support burden. You already know how to build one of those.

## What it is not

**Not a component library.** Components are the visible part. The system is the tokens, the patterns, the documentation and the decisions about when to use what.

**Not a Figma file.** Nor a repo. It is both, plus the agreement that they describe the same thing.

**Not finished.** A system that stops changing has stopped matching the product, and the product will route around it.
