Automated tools catch roughly a third of accessibility issues. That third should never reach review; the rest needs a person.

---

## Automate the mechanical third

```text
eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y    at authoring time — missing alt, bad ARIA
axe DevTools              browser extension, per page
@axe-core/playwright      in your e2e suite
Lighthouse CI             coarse, good for trends
```

In tests:

```ts
import { injectAxe, checkA11y } from "axe-playwright";

test("dashboard has no a11y violations", async ({ page }) => {
  await page.goto("/dashboard");
  await injectAxe(page);
  await checkA11y(page);
});
```

Put this on your component library's stories and it covers a wide surface cheaply. What it reliably catches: contrast failures, missing labels, invalid ARIA, duplicate IDs, missing document language.

---

## What needs a person

```text
is the tab order logical
does focus go somewhere sensible after an action
do the announcements make sense in context
is the error actually reachable and understandable
can the task be completed without a mouse
is the alt text meaningful, or just present
```

An automated tool will confirm every image has an `alt` attribute. It cannot tell you the alt text says "image1.png".

---

## The five-minute pass

For anything new, before it ships:

```text
1  tab through it — focus visible and ordered
2  resize to 320px — no horizontal scroll
3  zoom to 200% — nothing clipped or overlapping
4  DevTools reduced-motion — animations suppressed
5  axe scan — zero violations
6  turn on VoiceOver, use the main flow once
```

Steps 1 through 5 take about four minutes. Step 6 takes longer the first few times and becomes fast.

---

## Zoom is the forgotten one

WCAG requires content to work at 200% zoom, and 400% for reflow. Many layouts break well before that — fixed heights clip text, absolutely positioned elements overlap, sticky headers consume the viewport.

Zoom is also one of the most *used* accessibility features, far more common than screen readers. Testing it is cheap and catches real problems.

---

## Where to put the effort

Build the automated layer into CI so it never regresses, and spend your human attention on flows rather than pages: signup, checkout, the main task. A page can pass every automated check and still be impossible to complete end to end.
