Vercel describes design engineers as people who **skip the traditional handoff process**: a designer sketches the start and iterates with a design engineer in Figma or code to produce the final thing.

That only works if the designer wants you there. Which is earned, in specific ways, and lost in specific ways.

This chapter is about the social half of the role. It is not soft — it is the difference between being consulted early and being sent a finished file.

## The starting position

Most designers have been handed a long history of engineers saying "that's not possible" when they meant "that's not convenient."

You will be assumed to be that until you are not. The fastest way to prove otherwise is to be the person who says "yes, and here is what it costs" — or better, who builds it before the meeting ends.
