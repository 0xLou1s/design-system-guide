## The hiring signal

Vercel: *depth and quality of your experience matters more than years — portfolio and shipped work over tenure.*

This cuts both ways. There is no credential to acquire and no years-of-experience gate to wait out. There is also nowhere to hide: the work is the argument.

For someone switching from development, that is the favourable version. You cannot show five years as a design engineer. You can show something you built that is obviously good.
