Most of what you build is text. Which means typography is not a specialism you can defer — it is the majority of the surface.

The good news for an engineer: typography is the most rule-governed part of visual design. A handful of settings, applied consistently, get you most of the way. It is far more tractable than colour.

## The three that matter most

If you only fix three things, fix these:

```text
measure        45-75 characters per line
line height    ~1.5 for body, tighter for headings
scale          a fixed set of sizes, used consistently
```

Each is a single CSS property, and each is wrong in most interfaces built without attention. Getting these right lifts the perceived quality of a page more than any other change of comparable effort.
