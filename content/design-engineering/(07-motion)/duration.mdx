---

Almost everything is too slow. This is the single most reliable observation about motion in shipped products.

What feels elegant on the fifth viewing is tedious on the fiftieth — and your users are on the five hundredth.

---

## The ranges

```text
70-120ms     hover, focus, small state changes
120-200ms    dropdowns, toggles, local transitions
200-300ms    modals, drawers, larger surfaces
300ms+       rarely justified — usually a full page transition
```

If you are unsure, go shorter. A transition that is slightly too fast reads as responsive; slightly too slow reads as sluggish, and sluggish is the more damaging error.

---

## Scale with distance and size

A tooltip appearing 4px away and a drawer crossing the screen should not share a duration.

```text
small distance, small element     shorter
large distance, large element     longer
```

Same duration for both makes one feel abrupt and the other laggy. This is why a single `--duration-normal` applied everywhere feels wrong even when the value is reasonable.

---

## Exits are faster than entrances

Nobody wants to wait for something they have dismissed.

```text
enter    200ms
exit     150ms
```

Roughly 75% of the entrance duration is a good starting point. The asymmetry is invisible and the interface feels markedly more responsive.

---

## As tokens

```css
--duration-instant: 100ms;
--duration-fast:    150ms;
--duration-normal:  200ms;
--duration-slow:    300ms;
```

Four values covers product UI. As with spacing, the value of the scale is that you stop re-deciding — and that you can change the feel of the whole product by editing four numbers.

---

## The fifty-times test

The most reliable way to judge duration, and the reason prototyping in code matters:

**Use the interaction fifty times in a row.**

Anything that felt delightful on the first pass and tedious by the twentieth is too slow. You cannot get this from a design tool, a video, or a single click-through — only from repetition, which is how your users will experience it.

Do this before shipping any transition you are unsure about. It takes two minutes and the answer is never ambiguous.
